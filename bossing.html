Manual tracking

Use attempts since your last drop and zero drops obtained to describe a dry streak. Boss completions are lifetime totals, not automatically tracked streaks. With drops obtained above zero, the result describes overall drop luck instead.

Every independent attempt is a fresh roll. Being dry does not make your next drop more likely. Changing rates or multiple reward rolls may need a different model.
